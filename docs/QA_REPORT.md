# Showmark production QA report

Independent review of https://showmark.vercel.app (QIE Testnet, chain 1983) on 19 Nov 2026, roughly 16:15 to 17:15 IST. Bar: `docs/MASTER_BRIEF.md` section 8.

## Verdict

Not ready for judging yet, for one reason: the judges' desk is switched off in production. The hero's primary button, "Check in at the judges' desk", scrolls to a section that says the desk opens "as soon as the contracts are live on QIE Testnet", even though the contracts are live and event 1 already has a real check-in. That is a one-line environment fix, but until it ships the judge path stops there.

The rest is in good shape. Across 25 routes, 2 viewports and 2 themes (100 page loads plus the probes listed below) there was no horizontal scroll at 390 px. Visible copy had no em or en dashes, no emoji, no exclamation marks and no banned words. axe found no colour-contrast violations in either theme, and every page tested showed a visible keyboard focus ring. The 404 page, the invalid door link and the camera-denied state all say what happened and what to do next. Mobile LCP is under 2.5 s on both measured routes. The passport, stamp and receipt design language holds up and does not look templated.

Seven issues are major. Two are wrong numbers on public pages: the traction page shows Showmark at 400% of a day's chain transactions, and settled money shows as $0.00. The SDK docs point `relayUrl` at a domain someone else owns. The check-in route ships about 226 kB of JavaScript against a 170 kB budget. Some pages occasionally hang while loading. A remote judge has no door code to scan. A shared dotted-leader `<dl>` component fails axe on five pages.

Counts: **1 blocker, 7 major, 19 minor.**

## Method

- Playwright 1.65.1 (Chromium) at exactly 390x844 (mobile, touch) and 1440x900, with `prefers-color-scheme` emulated as light and dark. Every route was captured as a full-page JPEG (pages taller than 6000 px are cut at 6000 px) and I looked at each one. Desktop images were then scaled down to 1000 px wide to keep the folder under 15 MB.
- I injected axe-core 4.10.2 from cdnjs on every load and kept only serious and critical violations. I tabbed four times from the top of each page at 1440 light and checked the computed outline. At 390, I flagged interactive elements smaller than 24x24 px.
- A copy scan checked visible `innerText` on every load for U+2013/U+2014, `!`, emoji (Extended_Pictographic) and the banned list. The only hit was `!presence.hasAttended` inside an SDK code sample, which is a false positive.
- Links: I collected every internal and external link from all pages and checked each with curl.
- Lighthouse 12.6.1, mobile form factor with default simulated throttling (Slow 4G, 4x CPU), screen set to 390x844, on `/` and `/c/garbage`.
- Camera denied: Chrome with a fake media device and `--deny-permission-prompts`. Headless Chromium throws `NotSupportedError` instead of `NotAllowedError`, which wrongly shows the "no camera" message, so the screenshot was taken headed.
- **Production was redeployed during the run, at about 16:30 to 16:45 IST.** The contract addresses in the footer changed (Registry `0xd03B…802A` to `0xb975…5032`, Vault `0xFAA5…FCDe` to `0xb772…18b1`) and event 1 was recreated with new times. Screenshots from before the redeploy show the old addresses. `/`, `/traction`, `/e/1`, `/door/1` and `/p/…` were captured again after the redeploy, and every finding below was confirmed against the new deployment.
- No check-ins, wallet connections or transactions were attempted. Each route visit created a throwaway browser pass key in its own context, as the app does for any visitor. Nothing was signed or relayed.

## What passed

- No horizontal scroll and no clipped or overlapping text from overflow at 390 on any of the 25 routes, in light or dark.
- No em or en dashes, emoji, exclamation marks or banned words in visible copy anywhere, including docs, footer and page titles.
- axe found no `color-contrast` violations, and dark mode ("night desk") keeps the inks readable.
- Visible 2 px focus outlines on every page tested, plus a working skip link.
- Error and empty states are specific and say what to do next: `/c/garbage` ("This link is not a Showmark door code. Scan the QR code on the door screen again"), the 404 "Not admitted" ticket, camera denied, no pass yet, no stamps yet, nothing to withdraw, and the signed-out organizer and sponsor gates.
- Every number checked links to the chain. The home receipt is labelled "A specimen, not real data". All 27 internal links resolve (`/me` redirects to `/pass`). GitHub, explorer and qiewallet.me links return 200.
- The passport page, sponsor report and 404 ticket are portfolio grade.
